Mirrors deposit(uint256,address,string). Funds are assigned to the active wallet and overwrite any previous balance.

3. Withdraw balance

Mirrors withdraw(). Advancing the block past the unlock point may trigger a predictable bonus path in the demo to illustrate the weak randomness issue.
